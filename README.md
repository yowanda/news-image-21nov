One image summing up the world's news of **Saturday 21 November 2026** — a newspaper front page drawn with PIL: masthead and date, a lead story on Russia's biggest strikes of the year on Ukraine with missiles landing in Poland, and 23 more verified stories across regions and fields (markets, the ICJ genocide ruling on Myanmar, the Shenzhen Trump–Putin–Xi summit, UK–EU post-Brexit deals, COP31, a pancreatic-cancer drug breakthrough, GTA VI's record $3.8bn launch, BepiColombo at Mercury, and more), plus an "Also on 21 November" strip.

The image is built from reporting by the BBC, The Guardian, Al Jazeera, France 24, PBS and UPI as published on 21 November 2026. `main.py` copies `entry.png` to the output directory byte for byte, deterministically, with no network.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
```
